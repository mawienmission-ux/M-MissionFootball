Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 22, 2026

M.MissionFootball ("we", "us", or "our") provides football information about leagues, clubs, fixtures, results, standings, players, and news through our website and app. This policy describes information processed when you use these services.

Information we process

  • Public football content: league, club, fixture, score, standings, player, news, and image/logo information entered by authorized administrators. This content is intended to be visible to other users.
  • Administrator account information: an administrator's email address and authentication information are processed by Supabase Auth when the administrator signs in. We do not store the administrator's plain-text password in the app's football content.
  • Browser and device storage: the website may store football data and preferences in browser local storage, keep an administrator sign-in session in session storage, and cache website files for offline or faster loading. You can clear this information using your browser settings; clearing it may sign you out or remove local preferences.
  • Technical request information: our hosting and backend providers may process standard connection and diagnostic information, such as IP address, request time, and browser/device details, to deliver, protect, and troubleshoot the services.

How we use information

We use this information to operate the website and app, display shared football content, authenticate authorized administrators, save administrator changes, maintain service security, and resolve technical problems. We do not use the services to collect precise location or contacts.

Storage and service providers

Website hosting is provided by Render. Shared data and administrator authentication for configured versions of the service are provided by Supabase. Information is sent to these providers as needed to operate the service and is subject to their privacy and security practices:

  • Render Privacy Policy
  • Supabase Privacy Policy

Public football content can be read by visitors. Do not enter private or sensitive personal information into public league, club, match, player, or news fields.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Public football content is shared with website and app visitors. We may use the service providers listed above to host and operate the services, or disclose information where required by law or necessary to protect users and the services.

Retention and security

Public content remains available until an administrator changes or removes it, or the service is discontinued. Authentication data is handled by Supabase for as long as needed to provide administrator access. Browser-stored information remains until it expires or you clear it. We use access controls and provider security features, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Children

The services are intended for general football audiences and are not designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can review the request.

Your choices and requests

You may clear browser storage at any time. Administrators can request help with their account or personal information, and authorized administrators can update or remove public content. Contact us using the email below. We may need to verify your request before taking action.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our services or practices change. The effective date at the top of this page shows when it was most recently revised.
